# Amberwind

Core verb: bound, then fan out into a soft glide. A coral moth-fox gathers four amber seeds on a floating wind garden.

Controls: WASD moves in screen directions; Space jumps; hold Space while descending to glide; Shift dashes in the movement or facing direction. Jump is edge triggered, with coyote time and buffering. Falling respawns immediately; collected seeds persist.

Win: collect all four gems. No terminal loss. The first seed teaches running, the next two encourage turns and dash; the far seed rewards a jump/glide. The compact island is the entire slice.

Feel targets: 2.0–2.3 unit apex, 0.40–0.46 seconds to apex, strong 0.72 air control, 3.2 unit dash in 0.18 seconds. Compare spring (10/24 jump/gravity) against brisk (9/26); keep spring if the extra hang time helps readable wing deployment. Glide caps descent near 1.8 units/sec.

Visual direction: mint scalloped land stretched along the travel axis, layered plum basalt tapering below, peach parasol trees, ivory path stones, amber crystals, lavender sky and distant cloud islands. Creature: coral body, cream muzzle, long antenna ears, turquoise fan wings. Warm key and cool fill, PBR, soft shadows, restrained bloom, SMAA and ACES. Camera follows with damping, keeping the forward garden visible.

Bound: four gems, one island, one creature; no combat, menus, or extra levels.
