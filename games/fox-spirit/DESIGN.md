# Fox Spirit — color awakening smoke

See `docs/vision.md`.

- **Hero:** Tiny wispy multicolored fox spirit (fully saturated).
- **World:** Starts monochrome. Temporary color aura/trail follows the fox and fades.
- **Payoff:** One shrine permanently floods the island with color.
- **Controls:** WASD + Jump + Dash; hold Jump in air = glide.
- **Win:** Activate the shrine (gems optional juice along the path).
