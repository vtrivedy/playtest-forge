# Kickable leaf piles

Phase-2 "make the game great" (Kit :1391: the empty stretches, moment-to-moment play). The meadow jogs and the
lanes are walked a lot and nothing there answers the fox. A few mounded piles of fallen maple leaves sit beside the
paths; run through one and it bursts into a swirl of leaves that flutter down where they fall. Dash through and
the burst is bigger. The scattered leaves can be kicked again. It's a toy, not a task: no counter, no reward.

- **Where:** 8 piles, beside the canopy → grove jog, by the gate, the plaza's west side and the lane toward H-a;
  never on a collider, never blocking (no colliders at all).
- **Kick:** a resting leaf within 0.8 m of the fox (1.4 m dashing) launches when the fox moves faster than
  2.2 m/s: away from the fox and up (dash ×1.6), spinning; it falls with heavy air drag and a side-to-side sway,
  lands flat on the ground and rests there.
- **Look:** the same maple leaf mesh and warm litter palette as the ground litter, painted by the aura like
  everything else.
- **Landing:** a landing faster than 4 m/s (off the canopy, a heron fling, a long glide) puffs the leaves out within 1.5 m.
- **Sound:** one soft leaf rustle (`soft-leaf`, quiet) per burst, at most every 0.4 s.
