# Creature Island (reference smoke)

Spyro-inspired **original** creature platformer prototype — one floating island.

- **Core verb:** Bound — run, jump, glide, dash as a small dragon-like creature.
- **Controls (simple):** Move + Jump + Dash. Hold Jump in air to glide. Combos emerge from timing.
- **Win:** Collect all gems (5) within 60s.
- **Lose:** Fall off the island (respawn, gem progress kept).
- **Feel focus:** Satisfying jump arc, air control, glide as a power fantasy, snappy dash.
- **Visual:** Stylized PBR island, sky gradient, bloom, soft shadows, juicy gem collect.
