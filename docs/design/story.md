# Autumn Fox — Story

> **One line:** *A world that refused to let autumn end was pressed flat and still, like a leaf in a book. A quiet fox of ember and fallen leaves walks through it, warms it back, and lets the year turn again.*
> **Tone:** melancholic, beautiful, mostly wordless (Ghibli stillness · Ōkami restoration · Journey companionship). Grief and tenderness, not good vs. evil.

Companion docs: [`game-design.md`](game-design.md) · [`world-1.md`](world-1.md) · [`art-direction.md`](art-direction.md). Names below match `world-1.md` exactly.

## 1. Premise and tone

The world was always meant to turn: ember autumn, then frost, then pine-dark winter, then the long night that comes before spring. Long ago, someone could not bear the leaves falling, so they **kept** the season.

Now the world is a faded photograph. Leaves hang motionless in the air, halfway down and never landing. Villagers stand like sepia statues mid-step. Rivers are dry ink. Everything is preserved, and nothing is alive.

**Tone rules**
- **Sad things are gentle.** Enemies are ash creatures, lost rather than wicked. Defeating one frees it.
- **Beauty earns silence.** Big moments (Seedwells, the flood) get music and *no text*.
- **Nobody explains the whole story.** Pressed pages and Album fragments give it in pieces.
- **The fox never speaks**, never smiles, and never poses for the camera. It listens with its ears and answers with its tail.

---

## 2. The fox

A burnt-orange fox spirit with natural proportions, a cream chest, charcoal socks and narrow amber eyes. Its ear tips and tail dissolve into ember flame and falling leaves. It is **the last hearth-fox**. In the old story, hearth-foxes carried the fire that turns the year: wherever they ran, autumn ripened and then let go. When the Keeping came, every hearth-fox was pressed into the Album except one ember, which fell, and kept falling, for a very long time.

It is mysterious. Players should feel they are *accompanying* it more than controlling a mascot.

### 2.1 Name candidates

| Name | Meaning / sound | Pros | Cons |
|------|-----------------|------|------|
| **Akane** (茜) | "Madder red", the deep red of an autumn dusk sky (Japanese) | Elegant and Ghibli-adjacent; names the exact color of the turnaround | Common as a human name in Japan |
| **Sorrel** | Russet-red horse coat and an autumn herb | Soft, English-folk, earthy; reads "red-brown" | Slightly gentle for "mysterious" |
| **Wick** | The thread that carries a flame | Short, quiet, original; hints that the fox *carries* fire rather than *is* fire | Could read as cute if said in a cute way |
| **Tinder** | What catches the first spark | Fits the restoring fantasy | Dating-app association |
| **Rowan** | A tree with red autumn berries, folk-protective against ill magic | Mythic and protective | Gender-neutral human name; less fiery |

> Recommendation: keep the fox **unnamed in play** (Journey's "the traveler"). Use **Akane** or **Wick** as the title or Album name if a name is wanted.

## 3. Why the color was lost: The Keeping

**The Scribe** loved autumn more than anything: the light at four o'clock, the smell of leaf-smoke, the last warm day. Each year the Scribe pressed one perfect leaf in an Album to remember it, and each year the season ended anyway. Someone the Scribe loved ended with one autumn (deliberately left unsaid).

So one evening, as the first leaf fell, the Scribe opened the Album and **pressed the whole world into it.** That was **the Keeping**.

| What the Keeping did | How it shows in play |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| Pressed the season flat so it can never end | Sepia world, paper grain, foxing spots (`art-direction.md` §2.1) |
| Stopped time mid-fall | **Leaves hang motionless in the air**. Windmills are still and statues stand mid-step |
| Drew the life and color out into the Album's pages | Color exists only *in* the fox, which was never pressed |
| Hollowed anything that resisted into ash and dried ink | **Ash creatures**: Ashlings, Dust Moths, Blotters, Quill Knights, the Ash Warden |
| Sealed each realm's **Ember Wellspring**, the spring the season rises from | The realm can be warmed only by lighting its **Seedwells** and then the Wellspring |
| Drained the hub's **Great Well** dry | Lanternhollow's well fills one season at a time |

**The irony at the center:** in trying to keep autumn forever, the Scribe killed it. A kept leaf is a dead leaf. **Restoring autumn means letting it fall.** That is why the flood's payoff is every held leaf finally *falling*, and why the hues run **Ember → Frost → Pine → Night**: the fox doesn't save one season, it lets the year move again.

### 5.1 First NPC: Old Bramble wakes (hub 1:00–1:30)
The ember wake washes over the statue. Sepia cracks off Bramble like old paint. She blinks slowly.

> **Bramble:** Oh. Warm.
> **Bramble:** I was halfway through a sentence, I think. How long was I…? No. Don't tell me.

(The fox sits. Ears forward.)

> **Bramble:** The well's dry. The gate won't hold a passing warmth, little ember. Hold it. Like this.
> *(Prompt: JUMP + DASH)*

After the first Burst on the ghost-ink stairs:

> **Bramble:** There. Go on. Russetvale is past the gate. It was always first to turn.

## 6. World 1: Russetvale

### 6.1 Arrival
Portal arrival (0, 92). First frame: hanging leaves, golden haze, and on the far hill the **lone maple that still keeps a faint russet**. The camera holds for 2 s before input. No text.

### 6.2 Story beats along the critical path

| Zone | Beat | Text? |
|------|------|-------|
| A · Arrival Meadow | **Wen** stands frozen on the dock and wakes as the fox passes | 2 lines (§7) |
| B · Windmill Rise | SW1 lit: the mills turn for the first time in a hundred years. The sound of turning sails is the first *new* sound in the game | None |
| C · Reedmarsh | **Pockets** in the stilt hut, half-woken, keeps his door shut until the fox carries 150 embers' warmth | 2 lines |
| D · Amberwood | **Little Tam** hangs in the air between two crowns with wings half open, pressed mid-first-flight. The fox can warm her, but she stays held, because the Keeping is stronger here. The fox stays near her for a moment | None. The idle-near-Tam ear-droop animation carries it |
| Root bridge | The three color rivers meet at the hill's foot. The root bridge grows | None |

### 6.3 The Ash Warden: the sleeping hound
The Warden was the Wellspring's guardian hound. When the Keeping came, it **refused to leave its post** and kept guarding the sealed spring until its body turned to ash and ink. It still guards. It does not know there is nothing left to guard from.

- **It reads as grieving, not monstrous**: gaunt, slow when idle, and a soft low whine under its breathing. Its pounces are desperate rather than cruel.
- Each Burst briefly shows **a dim ember core** inside the cracks in its back. It still has a spark too.
- **The last Pounce Slam:** no explosion. The Warden staggers, turns once in a circle the way dogs do before lying down, and **crumbles into a sleeping-hound shape of ash**. The arena goes quiet.
- **Optional beat:** if the player stands still near the ash shape for 3 s, the fox lies down beside it. Ears flatten and the tail curls over its paws. The camera slowly lowers. Moving ends it. This is never prompted.
- Three hearth sockets light around the arena, the preview of Kindle.

### 6.4 The flood (W1 climax)
At the top of the spiral, the fox touches the Ember Wellspring's seal.

| t (s) | Story image |
|-------|-------------|
| 0 | Silence. The seal shatters, and its ink shards turn into maple leaves |
| 0.5–4.5 | Autumn rolls across the valley. Grass goes gold, windmills turn, the marsh glows copper |
| 2 | **Every held leaf in Russetvale begins, at last, to fall** |
| 3 | In Amberwood, Little Tam drops, opens her wings, and **flies** |
| 4 | The sun sinks toward evening. Birds lift and fly south |
| 6 | Little Tam lands on the fox's back. Leaves fall around them. The fox turns its head toward her; the tail lifts |

**After the flood**, the camera finds a far islet (−120, −95, 50): **the Scribe**, small with distance, Album held close. **One pressed leaf slips from the pages and drifts away.** The Scribe does not reach for it. Cut.

Under the lone maple, the Warden's ash shape is now covered in russet leaves. If the player visits, the leaves rise and fall slowly, as if breathing.

### 6.5 Return to Lanternhollow (W1 ending)
- The fox steps through the portal at dusk. **One leaf of the Great Well's stone maple crest warms to ember**, and the well's bottom shows a thin gleam of water.
- The south-east quarter of the village (houses, cart, two trees) is now autumn, and lanterns light on their own.
- Bramble, Hollin and Pockets (now at the hub cart) each have one line.
- The **Frost seal** (52, −30) breathes a little cold mist. The fox's breath shows once. Cut to the Album: a new page, *Russetvale*, painted in autumn watercolor.

> **Bramble:** It's falling again. The leaves. I'd forgotten that was the beautiful part.

## 7. Quiet NPC dialogue

**Rules:** at most 2 lines per visit and at most 25 words per visit. No exclamation marks, no jokes at the world's expense, no tutorials in dialogue beyond Bramble's single prompt. NPCs talk *to* the fox and never expect an answer.

| Speaker | Trigger | Line |
|---------|---------|------|
| Bramble | Idle near her (hub, pre-W1) | "Sit a while, if you like. Nobody's in a hurry anymore. That's the trouble." |
| Bramble | Holding ≥ 100 embers | "You carry the warmth well. Like your kind used to." |
| Hollin | First visit | "Hm. A hearth-fox. I had a page that said you were all gone." |
| Hollin | Pressed page #1 (H1, post-W1) | "Their handwriting. *'Kept, so it will never end.'* They always did press too hard." |
| Hollin | Pressed page #2 (S3) | "*'The leaves won't fall now. Why does it feel like they're still falling?'*" |
| Wen | Wakes on the dock | "Three springs feed the hill. Light them, and the big one might remember itself." |
| Wen | Repeat | "Patience. The water's been still a long time." |
| Wen | After the flood | "Hear that? Current. I'd forgotten the sound." |
| Pockets | < 150 embers | "Can't open for a chill. Come back warmer." |
| Pockets | ≥ 150 embers | "Ah. That's warm. Go on in. Mind the little one." *(Lost Kit #3)* |
| Pockets | Hub cart, after W1 | "I collect lost things. You collect them better. Here. Wear the color." *(russet wake tint)* |
| Little Tam | — | Never speaks. One soft chirp when she lands on the fox |
| The Scribe | — | **Silent in W1.** First line, W4: "I only wanted it to stay." |

### 7.1 Lost Kits
Each Lost Kit is a tiny ember kit asleep in sepia. When the fox finds one, it lies down beside it for 1.5 s and the kit's color returns. The kit then appears curled in the hub spawn leaf-drift, sleeping. **It is never explained.**

## 8. Arc beyond W1 (outline)

| World | Hue | Emotional note | Story turn |
|-------|-----|----------------|------------|
| W1 | Ember | Remembering warmth | The world can still move. The first leaf slips from the Album |
| W2 | Frost | Letting go is cold | The first thing restoration brings is loss: frost ends autumn. Villagers are sad *and* relieved |
| W3 | Pine | Endurance | Evergreens never let go and never needed to. Hollin reveals the Scribe was her teacher |
| W4 | Night | Grief | The Scribe is met in the long night. The fox lies beside them. The Album opens and every page falls like leaves |
| Epilogue | (Spring hinted) | Release | The Great Well fills. First green bud, seen only once and for a second. The Warden (perhaps) wakes |
